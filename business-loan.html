Who can apply

  • Self-employed proprietors, partnerships, Pvt. Ltd. companies, and MSMEs.
  • Applicant age between 21 and 65 years.
  • Minimum business vintage of 1 to 2 years in the current enterprise.
  • Minimum annual business turnover of ₹10 Lakh+ reflected in bank statements.
  • CIBIL / credit score of 675+ (725+ preferred for lowest interest slab).

Documents needed

  • Identity & Address Proof of promoters/directors (PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport).
  • Business Registration Proof (GST Certificate, Udyam MSME Registration, Partnership Deed / COI).
  • Last 6 to 12 months’ operative current bank account statements.
  • Last 2 years’ ITR with computation of income, Balance Sheet & Profit/Loss statement.

Before you apply

  • Ensure all GST returns and bank statements are up to date for faster sanction.
  • Check your credit score — a score of 750+ unlocks the lowest rate with Axis Bank & Lendingkart.
  • Compare processing charges and foreclosure terms before signing the loan agreement.
